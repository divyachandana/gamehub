**[Facility name]** · **[Unit, e.g. memory-care wing]** · Version **[date]**

## Why we use it

At night, one or two staff members may care for a whole wing. Some residents living with dementia get
up and try to leave, which can be dangerous, especially outdoors at night. NightWatch helps night staff
notice earlier when a resident has left their room or is near an exit, and **keeps alerting until a staff
member responds**. It supports our staff; it does not replace them, their judgement or our procedures.
It is not a medical device.

## What it uses

- **Door and window sensors** on exits: open or closed.
- **[If used]** bed sensors (in bed or not), movement, and heart rate from a watch the resident wears.
- **[If used]** a camera in **[the hallway outside the resident's room / the front entrance]**. Cameras are
  never placed inside bedrooms or bathrooms. **[Adjust to your setup.]**
- Basic resident details staff enter: a code and name, room, and what has helped calm the resident before.

## What it does **not** do

- **It does not record video or photos.** Each camera picture is checked in the laptop's memory for where
  people are, then thrown away straight away. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
- It does not recognise faces or identify who someone is. It only knows *a person* crossed the doorway
  line, and which camera saw it.
- Staff screens show **a dot on a floor plan, never video**.
- It does not send anything to the internet or to the company that made it.
- **[In the version described here] it does not call anyone automatically.** Emergency contacts follow our
  existing procedures. **[If you connect it to a phone or nurse-call system, say what is sent: alert text only.]**

## Where it is processed

On **one laptop inside the building**, at the nurse station. Staff phones connect to it over our own
Wi-Fi, and only phones that staff have paired with a PIN can see or answer alerts. It works without internet.

## How long information is kept

- **Camera pictures:** not kept at all (fractions of a second, in memory).
- **Positions from the camera:** only the last few seconds.
- **Tonight's alerts, event log and draft care notes:** kept on the laptop so a restart doesn't lose them,
  and replaced as the next night begins. Staff confirm the draft notes; confirmed notes go into the
  resident's care record and are kept under our normal record-keeping policy: **[retention period / policy name]**.
- **Shift report:** **[how long printed or saved reports are kept, and where]**.

## Your choices

- Each camera can be **switched off**, or set to watch **only at night** ([21:00–05:00]). "Off" turns the
  webcam off completely; nothing is analysed. Every switch is logged with who made it.
- You can ask us to set a camera near your relative to night-only or off, or not to use a watch or bed
  sensor for them. We'll explain what that means for how quickly staff can be alerted, and agree it with you.
