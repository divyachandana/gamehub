    Scenarios

    Cameras · privacy switches

    Off or paused: frames are dropped on arrival, never decoded or analysed. Every switch is logged with who did it.

    Contacts · who gets called

    OFF = dry run: every contact is marked "simulated" and nothing leaves this laptop. Turning it ON will really call, text, push or iMessage the contacts below (never emergency services). Resets to OFF on every server start.

    Really send to these contacts?

    Phone numbers in international format (+15551234821). Emergency and short numbers (911, 112, 999…) are refused: emergency services are always simulated. Family contacts are only told about their own resident.
